Privacy Policy

Doctor Meets Lawyer · Last updated: August 22, 2026

Doctor Meets Lawyer ("the App", "we", "us") provides a cross-industry conversation platform where professionals can meet, discuss topics, and chat with one another. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Account and profile information

  • Email address and password — required to create an account and sign in. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider.
  • Profile details — username, profession, gender, age, bio, and avatar image that you choose to provide.

Content you create

  • Topics and chat messages — the discussion posts you publish and the direct messages you send or receive.
  • Block list — the users you choose to block.

Usage and device information

  • Push notification token — a device identifier used only to deliver message notifications when you opt in.
  • Last seen timestamp — used to display your online presence to other members.
  • Premium membership status — whether your subscription is active and its expiry date.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and operate the App: topic feed, direct messaging, user profiles, blocking, and notification delivery.
  • To manage premium membership purchased through the App Store.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling for advertising purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and server-side functions. See Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — processes subscription management and entitlement verification. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Apple — handles App Store payment processing and may provide us with transaction information related to your subscription. See Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Expo — used to deliver push notifications. See Expo Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data while your account is active. You may delete your account at any time, which removes your profile and personal information. Some records may be retained for a limited period where required by law or for security purposes.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is rated 17+ and is not intended for children under the age of 17. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent change. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: richardchung_0907@hotmail.com
